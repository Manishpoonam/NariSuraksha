## 1. Welcome & Elevator Pitch (In Simple, Human Language)

> **"If you or a friend are ever threatened with private photos or videos online, your life is NOT over. You did nothing wrong. You are not alone, and there is a safe, zero-shame way out of this."**

### What is NariSuraksha?
NariSuraksha (*"Women's Safety"*) is a free, instant emergency response web companion designed specifically for women and girls in India facing:
- Non-consensual intimate image (NCII) abuse
- Blackmail and extortion ("Pay money or I will send your photos to your parents/college")
- Fake social media accounts and morphed/AI deepfakes
- Relationship harassment and digital stalking

### What makes it different from any other website?
1. **100% On-Device Privacy (Zero Servers):** 
   This website has **no user accounts**, **no database**, and **no server saving your photos, chats, or personal details**. Everything you type or test stays trapped inside your phone or laptop’s temporary browser memory. When you close the tab or press "Purge", it evaporates completely.
2. **Instant Stealth Disguise (Panic Shield):** 
   If someone suddenly walks into your room or demands to see your phone, you don't have to scramble to close tabs:
   - On Desktop: Press the **ESC** key once.
   - On Mobile: Swipe with **3 fingers** across the screen.
   - The screen immediately changes into an authentic-looking **"UPSC General Studies Revision Notes & Daily Planner"** page.
   - Even if the intruder presses the browser **Back button**, it is securely trapped so the real safety portal is never accidentally revealed!
3. **Zero Shame, Full Legal Immunity:**
   Under Indian law (**Section 73 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023**), publishing or sharing a victim's name or identity is a serious criminal offense punishable by up to 2 years imprisonment. You are protected by law, and police cannot disclose your identity.

3. **Don't Immediately Delete or Block:** If you block the perpetrator or delete your chat right away, you delete the only legal proof you have. Capture screenshots with battery status, timestamps, and usernames visible first.

## 3. Technical Architecture Explained Simply

When you present to NGOs, tech leads, or college faculty, they will ask: *"How do we know this website is safe? Where does the data go?"*

Here is your bulletproof explanation:

| Technical Feature | How It Works Under the Hood | Why It Keeps Women Safe |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Zero-Server Architecture** | The app runs entirely in client-side React inside the user's browser. | No user data, passwords, or files are ever sent to an application database or backend server. |
| **No Analytics / Zero Telemetry** | Google Analytics, trackers, Facebook pixels, and session replay scripts are strictly prohibited. | Nobody can trace what page a victim visited, what she drafted, or when she visited. |
| **Perceptual Hashing** | Converts pixel patterns into mathematical hashes locally using the HTML5 File API and client cryptography. | Photos never travel over the internet. Only non-reversible math fingerprints are shared with takedown networks. |
| **Emergency Camouflage** | ESC key (desktop) or 3-finger swipe (mobile) activates a realistic study notes screen and pushes multiple history entries. | If an abusive partner or family member grabs the device, hitting the Back button stays trapped in the study disguise. |
| **Instant Footprint Purge** | One-tap button flushes browser local cache, form drafts, clipboard, and session memory. | Leaves zero digital breadcrumbs on shared family phones or cyber café computers. |
| **Client-side PDF Generation** | Generates court-ready e-FIR drafts using `jsPDF` completely inside browser memory. | No documents are uploaded to cloud servers or temporary PDF converters. |

## 4. Presentation Speech / Workshop Script for NGOs & Counselors

*(Feel free to read this verbatim or adapt it for workshops at schools, colleges, or women's shelters)*

> *"Good morning/afternoon everyone.*
> 
> *Today, we want to talk openly about something that affects thousands of young women across India every day: cyber blackmail, leaked photos, and online extortion.*
> 
> *When someone threatens to leak a private picture or video, the first feeling is overwhelming terror: 'My life is ruined. My family will disown me. The police will blame me.'*
> 
> *I am here to tell you three undeniable facts:*
> * **Fact #1: You did nothing wrong.** Trusting someone in a relationship is human. Betraying that trust and extorting you is a cognizable criminal offense.*
> * **Fact #2: Paying money or begging never works.** Extortionists want control and money. The moment you pay, they realize you are frightened, and they will demand more.*
> * **Fact #3: Indian law is on your side.** Under Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita Section 73, any person who reveals your name or identity faces 2 years in prison. You have the right to have your statement taken by a female police officer under BNSS Section 173.*
> 
> *To help you navigate this safely, we have **NariSuraksha**. It is a completely private, on-device digital response toolkit that you can open on any phone.*
> 
> *You don't have to create an account. It doesn't save your photos. If someone walks into your room, swipe the screen with three fingers, and it instantly disguises itself as UPSC study notes.*
> 
> *Remember this number: **1930** (National Cybercrime Helpline) and **112** (Police Emergency). Bookmark this site. Share it with your sisters, friends, and hostel mates. Knowledge is our strongest armor."*

## 5. Quick Helpline Cheat-Sheet

| Emergency Channel | Number / URL | Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **National Cybercrime Helpline** | **1930** (Toll-Free, 24x7) | For stopping cyber extortion, blackmail & financial cyber fraud |
| **National Cyber Reporting Portal** | **cybercrime.gov.in** | Official portal for filing e-FIRs and NCII takedowns |
| **National Police Emergency** | **112** (All-India) | For immediate physical danger, stalking, or domestic violence |
| **Women in Distress Helpline** | **1091** / **181** | Specialized women police desks and state helplines |
| **National Commission for Women** | **7827-170-170** (NCW 24x7) | Legal support, grievance escalation, and counseling |
| **Childline & POCSO Protection** | **1098** (Toll-Free) | Strict statutory protection for minors under 18 years |
| **Mental Health Tele-Counseling** | **14416** (Tele-MANAS) | Free, 24x7 government psychological counseling |
| **Free Legal Aid (NALSA)** | **15100** (Toll-Free) | Free legal defense and advocates under Legal Services Act |
| **StopNCII Platform** | **stopncii.org** | Free non-profit hashing tool to block intimate images |
